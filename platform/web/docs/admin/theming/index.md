# Your Community's Theme

You can customize the look of your OpenPeeps community through robust set of configuration options.  Out of the box, OpenPeeps comes with two themes:  a Light Theme, with a white background and light-colored navigation components, and a Dark Theme that has light-colored text on a dark background.  

Within this framework, you may change the highlight color, imagery, and font for your community.
