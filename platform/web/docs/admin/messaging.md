# Messaging

The text your community shows to members and visitors — tagline, welcome
content, and community guidelines — is managed from **Administration →
Configuration → Community**.

<div style="height:20px"></div>

## Tagline

A brief description of your community shown on the login / sign-up page,
right below your logo.

Configure it at [Community Info](/admin/configuration/community/info).

<div style="height:20px"></div>

## About Page

Shown to visitors who aren't logged in yet — this is often the first thing a
prospective member reads about your community.  If you allow open signups to your community, this page appears before members can sign up. 

Supports Markdown formatting.

Configure it at [About Page](/admin/configuration/community/about-page).

<div style="height:20px"></div>

## Welcome Page

Shown to new members as part of their onboarding, after they join.  It can also always be accessed from the "Welcome Page" navigation item.

Supports Markdown formatting.

Configure it at [Welcome Page](/admin/configuration/community/welcome-page).

<div style="height:20px"></div>

## Welcome Email

The first email new members receive from your community. Add your own
information alongside the default onboarding content.

Supports Markdown formatting, though not all email clients render every
Markdown feature consistently.

Configure it at [Welcome Email](/admin/configuration/community/welcome-email).

<div style="height:20px"></div>

## Code of Conduct

The rules and expectations for your community. This page is public at
`/code-of-conduct` for anyone, including visitors who aren't logged in.

Supports Markdown formatting.

Configure it at [Code of Conduct](/admin/configuration/community/code-of-conduct).
